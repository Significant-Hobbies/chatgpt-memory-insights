## Design Health Score

**35/40 — strong, release-ready after the applied priority fixes.**

## Design Specificity Verdict

The page is strongly authored for Memory Map. Transit topology, route and stop
grammar, the evidence interchange, privacy boundaries, and “reflection, not
diagnosis” framing would not transfer unchanged to a generic AI product.

The dual-agent critique combined an independent visual review with an isolated
CLI detector and browser evidence pass. The detector returned no findings.

## What Is Working

- The hero makes the mechanism memorable by visibly connecting a repeated
  question, topic, changed memory, search destination, and source evidence.
- Privacy, model download, persistence, unsupported uses, and epistemic limits
  are separated instead of buried.
- The complete product inventory remains implementation-backed and navigable
  without a graph or client-side script.

## Priority Issues and Resolution

- **Resolved P1:** The initially expanded 36-item inventory became a long wall.
  Five native route disclosures now keep all capabilities available while
  showing one route at a time by default.
- **Resolved P1:** Mobile lost the desktop information architecture. A sticky,
  keyboard-accessible section stop bar now links to capabilities, privacy, and
  ZIP export guidance.
- **Resolved P2:** The desktop hero action could fall below a short first
  viewport. Type scale and vertical padding were tightened; the action is now
  visible at the reviewed desktop height.
- **Resolved P2:** Several navigation and resource links were below the
  preferred target size. Current targets are at least 44 CSS pixels high.

## Persona Check

- **Jordan, first-timer:** Can understand the input and outcome, jump directly
  to “Get the ZIP,” and follow official export instructions.
- **Riley, stress tester:** Can distinguish illustrative data, current

## Remaining Observations

- Exact model sizes and the 36-capability claim remain maintenance-sensitive
  and should be updated alongside implementation changes.
- The page intentionally favors exhaustive product truth over testimonials or
  benchmarks because no verified social proof exists yet.

## Questions

- Should a future release let visitors export a reviewed personal memory brief
